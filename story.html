THE VÉRONA STORY

A story rooted
in the land.

For nearly four decades, VÉRONA has been shaped by the soil, seasons and quiet traditions of Tuscany.

OUR BEGINNING

Wine begins long before the bottle.

VÉRONA began in 1987 with a simple belief: exceptional wine should express where it comes from.

Our first vines were planted among the rolling hills of Tuscany, where warm days, cool evenings and rich mineral soils create an environment naturally suited to thoughtful winemaking.

What started as a small family vineyard gradually became a collection of wines shaped by patience, craftsmanship and respect for the land.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Let the land
speak.

We believe great wine does not need to be forced. It needs time, attention and a place worth remembering.

From the first pruning of the vines to the final months spent resting in oak, every decision is made with balance in mind.

The result is wine that feels unmistakably Italian: expressive, elegant and connected to its origin.
