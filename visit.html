THE ESTATE

A place made
for slowing down.

Just outside Siena, VÉRONA sits among rolling vineyards and quiet Tuscan hills.

Our estate brings together the vineyard, cellar and tasting room in one place, allowing every visit to become a closer look at how our wines are made.

Come for the wine. Stay for the view.
